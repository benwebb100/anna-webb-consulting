About Anna

Twenty-five years partnering with leaders who want to do the work.

Her approach blends warmth with real accountability — helping leaders build the curiosity, compassion and courage modern leadership demands.

Anna helps leaders and teams perform at their best by strengthening resilience, wellbeing, connection and leadership capability. She partners with executives and organisations to create practical, lasting change that improves both individual performance and team culture.

With more than 25 years' experience across executive coaching, facilitation and leadership development, Anna is known for helping people build self-awareness, navigate complexity and lead with greater impact. Her approach combines evidence-based practices from positive psychology, neuroscience and emotional intelligence to develop the capabilities that matter most in modern leadership: curiosity, compassion, accountability, adaptability and trust.

Anna brings a warm, engaging style while also challenging clients to step up, stay accountable and translate insight into action. She has coached leaders across several of Australia's top 20 ASX-listed organisations and is recognised for her ability to quickly build trust, connect with diverse stakeholders and deliver measurable outcomes.
